About the Role As our Digital Marketing Intern, you'll work alongside our SEO & Publishing team members to see how a modern digital team operates: how organic search data shapes content decisions, how web content gets published and maintained, and how requests move from idea to live page. You'll leave with hands-on experience in SEO, web analytics, and content operations, plus a project you can showcase.   Key Responsibilities SEO Analytics and Organic Growth
  • Pull, clean, and organize organic search data from the Web Analytics tool and our keyword tracking into workbooks.
  • Help build recurring SEO reports that track organic traffic, rankings, and engagement, and turn the numbers into clear takeaways for the team.
  • Support keyword and competitive research using tools like Semrush, and flag content gaps and opportunities to grow organic visibility.
Website Content and Publishing
  • Publish and update pages in Content Management System following our templates and brand standards.
  • QA live pages after launch for accuracy, formatting, links, and schema.
  • Support on-page SEO checks, including title tags, meta descriptions, headings, and internal links.
  • Maintain digital assets, including images, and file naming.
Marketing Operations
  • Help manage publishing request intake by logging, triaging, and following up on requests in Asana.
  • Keep content trackers and project documentation current.
  • Build and update reporting slides and internal documentation, such as discoverability updates and process decks.
Ad Hoc Digital Marketing Projects
  • Support the team with time-sensitive digital marketing tasks as they arise.
Stretch Project
  • Pick a larger initiative, such as a content performance analysis, a competitor audit, or a refresh of an internal process, and present your findings to the broader team by the end of your internship.
Qualifications
  • Strong Excel or Google Sheets skills (pivot tables, lookups, clean data handling).
  • Comfort creating clear, simple slides.
  • Clear written communication and strong attention to detail.
  • Basic understanding of SEO and digital marketing acquisition fundamentals.
  • A proactive, organized approach: you follow up, keep things accurate, and manage multiple tasks.
  • Comfortable receiving feedback and working across teams.
  • Experience working across marketing, product, development, and management teams
  Nice-to-Have Qualifications
  • Experience with a CMS such as Contentful or WordPress
  • Familiarity with Semrush and Search Console
  • Experience with Asana or Wrike
  • Previous Internship experience considered an Asset
  • Previous Quality Assurance for web projects considered an asset
