About the Role Kensington's sales team is launching an internship program designed to give a motivated individual hands-on exposure to the full client sales cycle, from case file management through to client delivery, while working alongside our top-performing sales consultants. The Sales Intern will support day-to-day sales operations and contribute to larger, higher-impact projects aimed at improving the client experience and sales process.   Key Responsibilities Day-to-day support :
  • Shadow and work alongside a sales consultant on a day-to-day basis to gain first-hand experience of daily sales activities, client interactions, and the overall sales process
  • Review client case files to ensure all information is accurate, complete, and up to date
  • Connect directly with clients to fill in any information gaps needed to complete their file
  • Track and confirm that all booked services have been fully completed and delivered
Project work:
  • Review common complaint trends raised with the Travel Support Team (TST) to identify recurring issues and recommend process or communication improvements
  • Analyze the sales funnel to identify drop-off points and areas for conversion improvement, presenting findings and recommendations to the sales team
  • Support change management and communications activities, helping ensure updates reach the right people at the right time
  • Assist in change planning for sales team initiatives
Qualifications
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including comfort connecting directly with clients
  • Ability to work collaboratively across teams and levels
  • Interest in sales, travel, client service, or related fields
  • Self-starter with a proactive and solution-oriented mindset
