Work Term: January 2027 - April 2027    The Work

Reporting to the Director, Stress Testing, TRCO, within Risk Management, the incumbent will support the development and implementation of tools for reporting and disclosure purposes. This includes socializing requirements with stakeholders for the Macroeconomic Stress Test (MST), a regulatory requirement of the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) Canada. 

This position will execute on delivery in the following areas:  

Stress Testing – Develop and implement reporting deliverables for the Stress Testing program, including advancing the enterprise’s stress testing capability through tool building and AI implementation. Also, further enhance the ability to use Stress Testing for decision making and risk management purposes. 

 

 

The Core Responsibilities!

  • Assist in the development, implementation and socialization of consolidation and reporting templates, including automating certain processes where possible. Templates include Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Capital, Leverage, Credit, etc.
  • Support the team in the overall executing of the MST program.
  • Working and disseminating key information to relevant stakeholders and responding effectively to their questions and concerns.
  • Assist in the preparation and coordination of the exercise submission to OSFI (Model Surveys, Pre-Management Action Results, Post-Management Action Results, Supporting Commentary, EWST and Plan submissions).
  • Assist in preparation for regulatory presentations, Internal Audit exams, and any related enquiries.
  • Assisting in coordination with key stakeholders, including Finance, Treasury, Credit Risk Stress Testing, and Business Units (Personal and Commercial) in completing enterprise programs. 
  • Support ad hoc activities if and when they arise. 

Let's Talk About You!

  • Master program candidate in Math, Economics, Accounting or related streams or 3rd/4th year undergraduate students with strong academic success.
  • Strong knowledge in economics, accounting, econometrics, risk modelling, statistics or time series analysis.
  • Strong technical skills and execution, including coding and AI experience.
  • Good oral and written communication skills, with confidence in delivering effective presentations.
  • Proactively identifies and resolves issues, escalating matters promptly to manager as needed.
  • Attention to detail and ability to review and recommend actions to solve business problems.
  • Strong organization, time management and multi-tasking skills; managing multiple deadlines at one time.
  • Strong analytical ability for product/portfolio monitoring and stress testing.
