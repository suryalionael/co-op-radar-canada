Reporting to the Senior Director, the People Operations and Payroll Co-Op supports various functions within the People department. They assist with HR and payroll processes and coordinate translation needs to ensure compliance requirements are met.

This is a 4-month position from January to April 2027. This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

In this role, you can expect to:

    Key Accountabilities

    People Operations Support

  • Assist with the new hire pre-boarding and onboarding process.
  • Filing and maintaining up-to-date and accurate employee folders.
  • Respond to general employee inquiries regarding HR matters.
  • Support day-to-day operations of the People and Talent Acquisition (P&TA) team.
  • Work closely with the People Team on ad-hoc projects and requirements.

    • Payroll Support

  • Assist with processing Canada and US payroll.
  • Help prepare and review statements of earnings, direct deposit files, and periodic payroll registers.
  • Assist in generating and filing standard documentation, such as Records of Employment (ROEs) or basic benefit forms.
  • Support period-end and year-end reconciliations, match payroll reports to general ledger accounts or bank statements, and compile statistical summaries.
  • Escalate complex payroll discrepancies or collective agreement interpretation issues to senior payroll staff.

Your background includes:

  • Is in 3rd or 4th year in a post-secondary Business program, preferably with a major in Human Resource Management, Marketing, Communications, or equivalent.
  • Fluency in French is an asset.
  • Is highly motivated with the drive to take on new challenges and proactively problem solve.
  • Is deadline-oriented with strong multi-tasking and organizational skills.
  • Has excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Technically savvy with the ability to learn new software quickly.
  • Strong willingness and motivation to learn with an inherent curiosity.
  • Skills in researching payroll and HR best practices and trends.
  • Proven track record of outstanding customer service experience.
  • Passionate about games and/or entertainment.
