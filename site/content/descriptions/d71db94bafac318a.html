Who you are:

We are always looking for amazing talent who can contribute to our growth and deliver results! Geotab is seeking a Marketing Intern, LatAm who will work on the development, and implementation of the marketing and communication plans for the Latin American region. If you love technology, and are keen to join an industry leader — we would love to hear from you!

What you'll do:

As a Marketing Intern, LatAm you will be the main point of contact for marketing team members regarding marketing projects that are related to the region. In this role, you will work closely with marketing, sales, agencies, and vendors. To be successful in this role, you will be a self-starter with strong written and verbal communication skills.

The successful candidate will also support the team that is responsible for developing Geotab’s online presence, managing events, communications and PR, and product launches. This role will include working with social media, event planning, and continuously improving SEO.
