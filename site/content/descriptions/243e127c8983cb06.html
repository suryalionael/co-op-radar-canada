Work Term: January - April 2027

The Intern, Credit Risk Scoring, supports the development, monitoring, validation, and optimization of credit risk and account management scorecards across the customer lifecycle. Through the analysis of customer, portfolio, and performance data, the Intern generates insights, reporting, and recommendations that support scorecard development, risk management, model governance, and business decision-making.

The Core Responsibilities of the Job:

  • Analyze customer data to identify trends, opportunities, and emerging risks across acquisition, account management, and fraud risk strategies.
  • Develop, maintain, and enhance recurring score monitoring reports, dashboards, KPI tracking, and analytical tools to support model governance and risk oversight.
  • Support the development, recalibration, and optimization of credit risk and account management scorecards through quantitative analysis and performance evaluation.
  • Perform scorecard performance monitoring using statistical measures.
  • Assist in model validation, challenger model testing, and performance assessments under the guidance of senior team members.
  • Produce ad hoc analyses, business insights, and management presentations supporting scorecard strategy reviews and model enhancement initiatives.
  • Apply analytical techniques, statistical methods, machine learning concepts, and data visualization tools to generate actionable business insights.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders across Credit Risk, Fraud, AML, Product, Marketing, Operations, and Model Risk Management to support cross-functional initiatives.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of analytical methodologies, model monitoring processes, reporting automation, and decision-support tools.
