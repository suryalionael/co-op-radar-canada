Role Summary

Reporting to the Director, Cyber Security Operations, this co-op role operates across security operations, network engineering, and AI-driven automation.

This is a hands-on role where you will work directly with real security alerts, incidents, network systems, and operational workflows. In parallel, you will help design and build automation and AI-assisted solutions that reduce manual work, improve investigation quality, and strengthen how security and network operations are executed day-to-day.

 

Why This Role

  • Work with real alerts, tickets, logs, and enterprise infrastructure
  • Gain hands-on experience across both security operations and networking
  • Build AI copilots, automations, and workflows that solve real operational problems
  • Contribute to improving investigation quality and reducing analyst workload
  • Help shape safe and practical enterprise use of AI

What You Will Accomplish

    Security Operations

  • Monitor and triage alerts across SIEM, endpoint, email, identity, and access systems
  • Investigate phishing, malware, suspicious logins, and security events
  • Collect evidence, validate findings, and clearly document investigations
  • Escalate incidents and coordinate with IT and other teams
  • Support vulnerability tracking, remediation validation, and advisory monitoring
  • Assist with access reviews, account changes, and security controls
    • Network Operations and Engineering
  • Monitor network performance, availability, and connectivity
  • Troubleshoot issues across LAN, WAN, VPN, and wireless environments
  • Assist with configuration of switches, routers, and network devices
  • Support network changes and maintain diagrams and documentation
  • Investigate network-related alerts and assist during outages or disruptions
    • Network Controls, Security, and Audit Support
  • Support segmentation reviews and validation of trust boundaries
  • Assist with firewall rule reviews and IPS tuning validation
  • Validate access controls and permissions against requirements
  • Support logging, monitoring validation, and dashboard review
  • Review VPN and remote access configurations
  • Assist with vulnerability remediation related to network infrastructure
  • Maintain documentation and support audit readiness and evidence collection
    • Ticketing, Workflow, and Documentation
  • Create, update, and close tickets across security and network activities
  • Maintain clear, structured, and audit-ready documentation in Jira
  • Track remediation efforts and follow up on outstanding items
  • Improve workflow consistency, categorization, and documentation quality
    • AI, Automation, and Operational Improvement (Core Focus)
  • Build automations to reduce repetitive work (ticket creation, enrichment, correlation, deduplication)
  • Develop AI-assisted workflows for triage, summarization, and investigation support
  • Create repeatable templates, prompt patterns, and investigation frameworks
  • Improve documentation quality and consistency using structured approaches
  • Evaluate AI limitations (hallucinations, missing evidence, overconfidence) and implement safeguards
  • Support AI-driven monitoring and operations tooling
  • Work with real data (alerts, logs, tickets) to improve operational efficiency
    • AI Projects (depending on term length and focus)

    You may work on one or more of the following:

  • AI-assisted ticket triage and enrichment
  • Standardize how alerts are converted into structured incident records
  • Improve consistency, speed, and accuracy of investigations
  • Investigation quality rubric and feedback loop
  • Define what “good” investigation output looks like
  • Create scoring and improvement mechanisms for analysts and AI outputs
  • Copilot / agent for SOC workflows
  • Build a small agent that performs a real task end-to-end (summarize, recommend actions, generate tickets)
  • Focus on reliability, guardrails, and measurable time savings
  • Safe enterprise AI playbook
  • Define guidelines for AI usage, data handling, and risk prevention
  • Help reduce data leakage and unsafe usage patterns
    • Safe AI and Governance
  • Support least-privilege access and scoped data usage for AI tools
  • Help define what data can and cannot be used with AI systems
  • Contribute to guardrails that prevent data exposure or misuse
    • Assist in creating internal guidance for safe and effective AI use
