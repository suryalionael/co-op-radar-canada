The Core Responsibilities of the Job:

  • Support the digital banking consumer awareness and acquisition programs across multiple marketing channels, collaborating with internal teams and external partners such as agencies, legal, channel management, operations, and finance.
  • Assist in tracking marketing budgets, campaign performance, and project timelines to help ensure initiatives are delivered effectively.
  • Contribute to the execution of marketing campaigns and help maintain consistent brand and product messaging across consumer touchpoints.
  • Support the marketing team in developing and launching engaging campaigns across traditional and digital media channels to drive awareness and customer acquisition.
  • Assist with marketing analytics and reporting by gathering data, monitoring campaign performance, and identifying opportunities for optimization.
  • Participate in brainstorming and research activities to help develop innovative marketing ideas that support customer growth objectives.
  • Support customer acquisition initiatives by helping coordinate campaign activities and tracking results against key performance indicators.
  • Research potential partnerships, referral opportunities, and emerging marketing channels that may help increase brand awareness and product adoption.
  • Stay informed on marketing, digital banking, e-commerce, and consumer trends, sharing insights and recommendations with the team.
