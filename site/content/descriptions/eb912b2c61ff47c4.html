About the Role We are seeking a dynamic and enthusiastic individual to join our Partner Relations – Procurement team as an intern supporting our hotels supply vertical, while also providing hands-on support to our Sales leadership. Our teams are responsible for account management, rate competitiveness and negotiation, business development, and procuring the right connectivity solutions across these supply partners. This internship offers hands-on exposure to how a leading luxury travel company manages, analyzes, and grows its supplier partnerships. You'll split your time between core Partner Relations work (40%), sales strategy (30%) and performance reporting, and a high-impact automation project support (30%).   Key Responsibilities Partner Relations 
  • Support day-to-day operations and communication with hotel supply partners alongside the team 
  • Research potential new supply partners, key contacts, and destinations across hotel vertical 
  • Run rate competitiveness analyses and mystery shopping to inform contract negotiations and pricing 
  • Prepare agendas, presentation decks, briefing materials, and follow-up logs for partner meetings and business reviews 
  • Summarize supply trends and market dynamics into clear insights on the competitive hotel landscape  

Sales Strategy Support 

  • Build and improve a centralized hotel team sales tracker 
  • Help maintain internal sales dashboards that track performance against key milestones 

Automation & Data 

  • Maintain hotel catalogues and partner databases and generate performance reports 
  • Use AI tools, including Claude, to automate recurring data updates and reporting 
  • Collaborate with Analytics, Product, Operations, and Sales to make sure your work fits how those teams operate 
Qualifications
  • Currently enrolled in a relevant academic program (e.g., Business Administration, Hospitality Management, Tourism, Finance, or related field) 
  • Basic knowledge of, or interest in, analytics, sales, and account management 
  • Strong analytical skills and comfort working with data, reporting, and spreadsheets, proficiency with Claude/ AI a plus  
  • Advanced Excel skills, including the ability to build complex spreadsheets, analyze large datasets, develop reporting tools, and create models to support sales performance analysis and strategic decision-making. 
  • Foundational to intermediate data modeling skills, with the ability to structure, organize, and interpret data to identify trends, evaluate performance, and generate actionable business insights. 
  • Strategic mindset, with the ability to help design strategies that balance competitiveness, profitability, and long-term value creation 
  • Comfortable in fast-paced, data-driven environments where priorities shift based on market conditions 
  • Strong verbal and written communication and interpersonal skills 
  • Ability to multitask and prioritize tasks effectively 
  • A proactive self-starter who isn't afraid to improvise or make independent decisions when needed
