Design Verification Engineer Intern (Toronto)

Location: Toronto, Ontario, Canada
Employment type: Internship

About Astera Labs

Astera Labs develops purpose-built connectivity solutions for rack-scale AI infrastructure. Our Intelligent Connectivity Platform brings together high-performance connectivity technologies for AI, cloud, server, and networking systems.

The role

As a Design Verification Engineer Intern in Toronto, you will help verify complex digital designs for connectivity products. You will contribute to simulation-based verification, create and run tests, analyze waveforms and logs, and help improve functional coverage and reusable verification infrastructure.

This internship is an opportunity to build hands-on experience at the intersection of digital design, computer architecture, verification methodology, and software tooling while taking ownership of meaningful verification work.

What you’ll do

  • Develop, modify, and run simulation-based tests for digital design and connectivity IP blocks.

  • Contribute to SystemVerilog and UVM-based verification environments, including tests, sequences, agents, scoreboards, monitors, and checkers.

  • Run individual tests and regressions, review build and simulation results, and triage failures.

  • Debug functional issues by analyzing logs, transactions, register activity, and waveform databases with industry-standard tools.

  • Add or update test definitions, regression lists, manifests, and scripts as verification requirements evolve.

  • Track functional and code-coverage results and help identify meaningful coverage gaps.

  • Work with reusable verification infrastructure, protocol agents, reset and interrupt components, register models, and scoreboards.

  • Explore assertions, formal verification, or emulation workflows when appropriate for the design and project.

  • Collaborate with design, architecture, emulation, and verification engineers to reproduce issues and communicate findings.

  • Document test intent, debug conclusions, verification procedures, and project progress.

What you bring

Basic qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical field.

  • Coursework, project, research, or hands-on experience with digital logic, computer architecture, RTL, or hardware verification.

  • Programming or scripting experience in SystemVerilog, Verilog, C/C++, Python, or a comparable language.

  • Familiarity with Linux development environments, command-line tools, and Git or another source-control system.

  • Strong analytical and debugging skills, with an interest in understanding complex hardware behavior.

  • Strong written and verbal communication, organization, and time-management skills.

  • A hands-on work ethic, curiosity, learning agility, and ability to take ownership of assigned deliverables.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience with SystemVerilog, UVM, constrained-random verification, assertions, or functional coverage.

  • Familiarity with simulation, waveform-debug, regression, or coverage tools such as VCS, Verdi, DVE, or equivalent tools.

  • Experience writing testbenches, sequences, scoreboards, monitors, checkers, or reference models.

  • Exposure to AXI, APB, PCIe®, CXL®, memory systems, fabrics, controllers, or other high-speed digital interfaces.

  • Familiarity with formal verification, emulation, RTL simulation, or register-abstraction-layer models.

  • Experience using Python, Tcl, or shell scripting to automate verification workflows and analyze results.
