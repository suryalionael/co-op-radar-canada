Reporting to the Assistant Controller, the Accounting Co-Op will assist the Accounting team with processing accounts payable, month-end reconciliations, and updating company records.

This is a 4-month position from January to April 2027. This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

In this role, you can expect to:

    Key Accountabilities

  • Process accounts payable including communicating with external and internal business partners, 
    validating, processing, and recording invoices.
  • Set up vendors in the systems.
  • Review internal expense reports along with corporate credit card reconciliations.
  • Record bank transactions and apply payment to invoices.
  • Prepare intercompany journal entries related to the recording of invoices.
  • Assist with the preparation of month-end journal entries and account reconciliations including but not limited to cash and fixed assets.
  • Prepare and update process documentation to ensure that certain accounting policies and internal procedures can be memorialized in writing.
  • Pulling sample requests for auditors.
  • Other ad-hoc tasks and/or projects as opportunities arise.

Your background includes:

  • Enrolled in a post-secondary institution seeking a Diploma or Degree in Accounting, Finance, or Business Administration
    Completion of fundamental accounting courses.
  • Pursuing a CPA designation is an asset but not required.
  • Practical general accounting experience in a professional or volunteer setting.
  • High attention to detail.
  • Strong communication, both written and verbal.
  • Solid proficiency in MS Office Suite (particularly in Excel), and Google Workspace/G-Suite.
  • Proven track record of being self-directed along with exceptional organization and research skills.
  • Flexible and willing to go that extra mile to gain more knowledge and experience.
  • Demonstrate the capacity to manage ambiguity and shifting priorities while remaining calm and controlled.
