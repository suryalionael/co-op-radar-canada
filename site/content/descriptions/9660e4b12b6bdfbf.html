In this role you will :

  • Develop product and packaging designs, mockups, and original artwork for apparel and consumer products.

  • Create merchandising assets for web and marketing channels, ensuring compliance with brand and IP guidelines.
  • Contribute to product line development through trend research in apparel, collectibles, and horror culture.
  • Collaborate with Marketing and cross-functional internal and external partners. 

What we are looking for :

  • Studies in industrial design, product design, fashion design, or graphic design, completed or ongoing
  •  A portfolio that includes product design work, such as apparel, packaging or objects; a modern horror aesthetic is considered an asset.
  • Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop.

  • Familiarity with the Dead by Daylight universe, an asset.
  • Experience preparing files for print or manufacturing, an asset.
