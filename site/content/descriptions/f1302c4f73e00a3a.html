Work Term: January - April 2027

The EQ Bank Public Relations & Communications team is seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Co-op Student to support corporate communications, media relations, executive communications, and reputation management initiatives across the organization.

This role offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience within a dynamic corporate communications function supporting a leading Canadian financial services organization. The successful candidate will work closely with communications professionals and business partners across the bank, contributing to high-profile campaigns, strategic announcements, thought leadership initiatives, media relations activities, and executive communications programs.

The position is well suited to a student who demonstrates strong writing, research, analytical, and project coordination skills and who is interested in pursuing a career in public relations, communications, journalism, or related fields.

The Core Responsibilities of the Job:

    Media intelligence and reporting

  • Monitor EQ Bank brands, campaigns, executives and competitor coverage through Meltwater.
  • Tag, organize and clean coverage data for monthly and quarterly reporting.
  • Pull reports, flag notable coverage and draft first-pass summaries, slides and internal updates.
  • Support the bi-weekly media coverage newsletter by gathering coverage and preparing short blurbs.
  • Prepare ongoing quarterly media reports and decks, including those going to Investor Relations, the Board, and being displayed internally on our office screens.

    • Owned channel amplification

  • Draft first-pass corporate and executive LinkedIn copy using approved messaging.
  • Support internal amplification across channels like Viva Engage and Teams.
  • Prepare post-launch recaps highlighting what went live, where and when.
  • Coordinate with Marketing, Legal & Compliance and external partners to support approvals.

    • PR materials and presentation support

  • Build first-draft slides for campaign proposals, coverage reports and campaign updates.
  • Pull screenshots, links and supporting materials for decks.
  • Format slides to align with brand templates and existing reporting style.
  • Draft concise summary emails and leadership updates.

    • Campaign coordination and execution support

  • Maintain run-of-show documents and minute-by-minute trackers for announcements and campaigns.
  • Track deadlines, approvals, stakeholder inputs and outstanding deliverables.
  • Coordinate with internal teams on visual asset briefs and campaign materials.
  • Take meeting notes, capture action items and send recap notes.

    • Media relations research and planning

  • Support media list development and research relevant journalists, outlets and beats.
  • Identify media trends and opportunities to support proactive pitching with agency partners.
  • Track awards, speaking opportunities, editorial calendars and relevant industry events.
  • Support executive briefing notes with background research and recent coverage.

    • Content and sponsored editorial support

  • Support briefs for sponsored content opportunities with media outlets.
  • Take first-pass edits on sponsored content drafts.
  • Consolidate internal stakeholder feedback and track timelines through delivery.
