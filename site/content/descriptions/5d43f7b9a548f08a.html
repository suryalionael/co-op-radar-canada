Job Title: Co-op/Intern, Accounting - Winter 2027

Location: Remote - Ontario 

Term: 4 months

Start Date: January 4, 2027

We’re looking for an individual to be a part of the Global Controllership team and help us with the accounting and reporting function. Reporting to the Manager, Corporate Accounting, this position supports full-cycle accounting, month-end close processes, financial reporting and audit activities with the finance team. This role is based remotely within the Greater Toronto Area. 

 

WHAT YOU’LL DO:

  • Support an accurate and timely month-end close process, including maintenance of the general ledger
  • Assist with full-cycle accounting tasks, including preparing reconciliations, analysis and schedules
  • Assist in preparing monthly, quarterly and annual accounting working papers and supporting schedules
  • Support the preparation of journal entries and analysis for assigned financial statement captions, with review by senior team members, to ensure accuracy of financial reporting
  • Assist with interim and year-end audits of financial statements of the group by preparing audit support and responding to auditor requests
  • Assist with the treasury function; support wire runs and day-to-day banking administrative duties
  • Support the preparation of monthly bank reconciliations across multiple entities, accounts and currencies, investigating and clearing reconciling items on a timely basis
  • Record bank fees and interest in the general ledger, and monitor bank account activity for unusual items
  • Support the Finance team with system implementations, integration and other special projects as required
  • Contribute to the documentation, standardization and improvement of processes and internal controls
  • Learn from senior team members and build technical accounting and systems expertise

 

 

WHAT YOU’LL NEED:

  • Currently pursuing a degree/diploma in Finance or Accounting
  • Experience or exposure in
    • multi-entity consolidation processes
    • accounting for foreign currencies
    • questioning the status quo and suggesting continuous improvements
  • Practical knowledge of US GAAP
  • Working knowledge of internal controls and best practice accounting processes
  • Ability to leverage technology and automation to drive process improvement and efficiencies
  • Experience working with finance systems and tools is preferred; NetSuite experience is an asset
  • Commitment to Results: consistently achieves results, demonstrating high performance, and challenging self to deliver results
  • Accountability: holds self accountable to meet commitments
  • Problem Solving: uses an organized and logical approach to find solutions to problems. Looks beyond the obvious to understand the root cause
  • Curiosity: shows up curious, seeks new inputs, and considers different perspectives

  • Solution seeker: You’re focused on tackling new challenges, solving problems, and moving the business forward—and you don’t wait to be asked.
  • Lifelong learner: You have a growth mindset – you’re here to learn, experiment, seek, apply, and provide feedback, share what works with your team, and move on from what doesn’t.
  • Resilient adapter: In the face of change and challenges, you bring a thoughtful, calm approach and a focus on finding new opportunities.

  • Critical challenger: You have the trust in your team to ask difficult questions in order to get to the best end result.
