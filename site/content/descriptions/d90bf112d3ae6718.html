About the Role We are seeking a creative and proactive Design Intern to support our internal Creative Team. Reporting to the Senior Art Director, this role offers hands‐on experience in supporting the overall creative process, from briefing to launch, with the opportunity to contribute to work that drives meaningful impact. You’ll play a role in helping the Design Team evolve the brand, elevate their work, and be more effective in their execution.

What you'll gain from the role:
  • Hands-on experience with real-world brand marketing campaigns.
  • Experience as part of a real-world creative team, as well as collaboration across our larger cross-functional team.
  • Insights into creative strategy, execution, processes, and time management.
  • Professional development and mentorship in a dynamic work environment.
  Key Responsibilities
  • Supporting Design Team across a wide range of projects and channels as directed, including resizing and adapting assets, preparing files for handoff, sourcing and organizing imagery, and making updates based on feedback.
  • Working on social content and email design.
  • Organizing and filtering visual asset libraries: logos, images, video.
  • Embracing documented brand standards to design in a way that is consistently eye-catching and memorable, using approved fonts, logos, and protocols.
  • Displaying strong attention to detail.
  • Collaborating in a productive, enthusiastic, and egoless way with all team members throughout the entire creative process.
  • Welcoming feedback on their work and being proficient at incorporating it with minimal oversight.
Qualifications
  • An online portfolio of strong design work.
  • Proficiency in Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign; experience with Figma, Asana, and Claude an asset.
  • Digital imaging and retouching skills. 
  • Understanding of good typography and composition. 
  • Certificate or bachelor’s degree in Design, Image Arts, Digital Production, or other relevant program, or working toward one.
