The Project Coordinator Co-Op keeps Kabam's Creative Services (CS) team organized and running smoothly. CS produces marketing creative (key art, UA ads, video, social assets) for multiple live and upcoming mobile titles, working with in-house artists and external vendors.

You will own the day-to-day upkeep of our ticket pipeline, asset library, and vendor tracking, and act as a first point of contact for marketing requestors. This is a hands-on operations role with real ownership, not shadowing.

This is a 4-month position from January to April 2027. This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

In this role, you can expect to:

    Key Accountabilities

    Production and Asset Management

  • Maintain the CS ticket board in Asana: triage incoming requests, keep statuses and due dates current, and archive completed work.
  • Check new tickets for completeness and follow up with requestors on missing information.
  • Review queued tickets with requestors regularly to confirm they are still active.
  • Organize and maintain the CS asset library in Google Drive, including folder structure and naming conventions.
    • Requestor and Vendor Coordination
  • Act as a point of contact for marketing requestors on ad-hoc requests, status questions, and delivery timing.
  • Track external vendor deliverables and timelines, and support invoice tracking as needed.
  • Flag scheduling conflicts, workload spikes, and risks to the Creative Operations Lead.
    • Reporting and Documentation
  • Prepare regular status and workload reports for the CS team.
  • Take notes and track action items in team and cross-team meetings.
  • Build and maintain a CS team wiki covering processes, conventions, and onboarding.
    • Licensor Approval Tracking
  • Maintain an internal tracker of licensor submission status and feedback for licensed-IP projects.
  • Support submission to IP stakeholders and licensor correspondence under guidance and alignment with the Creative Operations Lead and key stakeholders.
    • What You’ll Gain
  • Hands-on project management experience in a live game marketing pipeline, giving you real experience to build on if you pursue PMI certification (i.e. CAPM, PMP).
  • Direct exposure to working with external creative vendors, including scoping, scheduling, and invoicing.
  • A view into how marketing creative is made for licensed-IP mobile games, from brief to delivery.
  • Daily mentorship from the Creative Operations Lead, plus ownership of real systems the team depends on.
  • Practical skills in Asana, Google Workspace, and production reporting.

Your background includes:

    Required

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's degree program and eligible for a co-op term.
  • Strong organizational skills and close attention to detail. You notice when a file is misnamed or a due date doesn't add up.
  • Comfortable liaising directly with senior colleagues and external partners, clearly and professionally.
  • Working knowledge of spreadsheets (Google Sheets or Excel), such as sorting, filtering, and basic formulas.
  • Clear spoken and written communication in English.
    • Preferred
  • Interest in project and production management as a career, or plans to pursue a certification such as the PMI CAPM.
  • Experience with project management or organizational tools (Asana, Jira, Trello, Notion, or similar).
  • Experience designing or cleaning up a system, such as a shared drive, a club's files, or a tracking sheet.
  • Interest in tech, games, marketing, or creative production.
