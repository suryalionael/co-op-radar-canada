About Steel

Steel is building open-source browser infrastructure for AI agents and apps. We make it easy for developers to ship AI products that interact with the web using our Sessions API.

With over 7,000 GitHub stars, dozens of paying customers, and millions of sessions served monthly, we grew our platform 50x in 2025 purely through word-of-mouth and our open-source community. Backed by world-class investors, we're building a small, highly motivated, and talent-dense team that's shaping the future of how humans interact with the internet.

More on the problems we're solving: https://docs.steel.dev/overview/intro-to-steel

Applying

We care more about your creativity, taste, and initiative than a traditional résumé. Your application has two parts:

1. Your work. Share a link to something you’ve made. It could be a video, a design, a social account, or a side project.

2. A short video (5 minutes max). Pick one thing you made that you’re proud of and walk us through it: what it is, why you made it, and what you’d do differently. No fancy editing needed. Share it as a link we can open without requesting access (Loom, unlisted YouTube, or a Drive link set to “anyone with the link”).

If there appears to be a fit, we'll reach out to schedule an interview and discuss your work and ideas.
