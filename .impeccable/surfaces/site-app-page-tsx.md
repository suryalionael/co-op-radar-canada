# Jobs board (/) — and /companies, /companies/[slug]

Mode: Operate. Audience and job: see PRODUCT.md. The visitor scans open co-op/intern roles, filters to their school's term, province and length, checks the evidence, and leaves to apply on the employer's page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The board is a timetable of work terms. Age sits in the departure column, and term and length are service facts read straight from the posting. It refuses the category default: soft rounded cards on grey with a purple accent.

OWN-WORLD: A GO-transit green rail (#00703c) over an off-white ground (#f6f7f2), with near-black ink and timetable-yellow (#ffc72c) for highlight and status. Overpass (signage) for display, labels and numerals; Atkinson Hyperlegible for text. Square corners, 1px rules, and a black table head bar. Length is a solid green plate ("4 MO"). Company hiring months are a 12-cell service-calendar strip with a black frame.

STORY: The visitor sees what is open now, narrows it by term, province, length or search, opens a row to read the posting's own words, and applies on the employer's site. From Companies they see who is hiring now, who usually opens soon, and in which months.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The green rail carries the wordmark, the nav (Jobs, Companies, Programs, About) and the live counts. Below it, the headline sits left with a yellow highlight, and a one-line pitch sits right. A full-width search bar with Term / Province / Length selects follows. The timetable rows start by ~300px from the top, with the service calendar in a right column on desktop. The primary action is each row's Apply link.
